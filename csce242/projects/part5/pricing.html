Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden fees or auto-renew surprises?

None. We stand behind complete financial clarity. Our Pro plans are billed simple monthly or annually, and you can cancel anytime within your account settings with a single click.

What is the 5-minute free budget audit?

It is our proprietary visual diagnostic. It maps your high-level numbers securely inside your browser to locate immediate, simple wins in your cashflow without storing your bank logs.

Can I invite my family members to share my account?

Yes. Our Pro plans include multi-seat family options. You can allocate secure independent profiles for spouses, teens, or relatives to work through lessons sequentially.

Are the lessons structured by CFPs?

Absolutely. All our financial worksheets, calculators, index fund guides, and tax strategies simplified structures are co-designed and regularly reviewed by credentialed CFPs.

Do you store my personal banking credentials?

Never. MoneyWise is strictly an educational platform. We provide visual calculators and spreadsheets where you safely type in your high-level figures manually.

Is there a student or military discount?

Yes. We are dedicated to accessibility. Reach out to our team at support@moneywise.edu with credentials and we will happily set up a custom access track.
