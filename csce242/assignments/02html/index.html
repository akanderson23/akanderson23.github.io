South Carolina Gamecocks Football

Williams-Brice Stadium

The South Carolina Gamecocks play their home games at Williams-Brice Stadium in Columbia, right on the USC campus. The stadium holds over 77,000 fans and is known as one of the loudest places to play in the country, especially once the band starts playing before kickoff and the whole place is jumping.
